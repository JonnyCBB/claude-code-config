# Escalation and Authority Examples

## Authority Example

Ask whether private order history should be retained for 30 days or indefinitely, even
though neither choice changes the screen. Research which API exists instead of asking the
owner to pick a method. A library choice may be agent-owned only if its impact is low and
reversing it is easy.

## Vague-answer pivot

After two vague answers on one topic, say "Let's back up — I may be asking the wrong
question." Recap what you heard and ask what happened in a recent real example. For a
preventive idea or when no real example exists, ask the user to sketch an imagined
plausible scenario and what they would want to happen; do not imply it already happened.
Do not ask the same question with different words.
