# Lanzarote, Feb half term 2027 — which provider is genuinely cheapest all-in

**All prices retrieved live on 28 July 2026.** Spec priced everywhere: London Gatwick → Lanzarote (ACE), depart **Sat 13 Feb 2027**, **7 nights**, **2 adults + 1 child aged 7**, **All Inclusive**, 1 room. Package prices move daily — these are snapshots, not quotes.

---

## The answer

**Book easyJet holidays.** It is cheapest on the headline _and_ cheapest all-in, because its price already contains the two things the others charge for. You were right to worry about bags — loveholidays is the one that catches people out.

| Provider             | Cheapest All Inclusive hotel                    | Headline   | Hold bags                   | Transfers                   | **Landed total**         |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **easyJet holidays** | BlueBay Lanzarote 3*, Costa Teguise             | **£2,247** | **3 × 23kg included**       | **Shared shuttle included** | **£2,247**               |
| **loveholidays**     | Apartments LIVVO Morromar 3*, Puerto del Carmen | £2,277     | Not included — **+£305.94** | Not included — **+£51**     | **£2,633.94**            |
| **TUI**              | HL Paradise Island, Playa Blanca                | £3,277     | not verified                | not verified                | **≥£3,277** (see caveat) |

The easyJet and loveholidays headlines are **£30 apart**. The landed prices are **£387 apart**. That entire swing is bags and coach.

### The same-hotel test

**BlueBay Lanzarote is sold by both**, so this is a genuine like-for-like rather than two different hotels:

|                                       | easyJet holidays                        | loveholidays                                           |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| BlueBay Lanzarote, AI, 13–20 Feb 2027 | **£2,247**, bags and transfers included | **£2,637** headline, bags and transfers _not_ included |

Same hotel, same week — easyJet is £390 cheaper before loveholidays has added anything. Applying loveholidays' own add-on rates (£305.94 bags + £51 transfer, retrieved on its Morromar booking for the same easyJet flights) puts it around **£2,994**, i.e. roughly **£750** more for the identical room. _That £2,994 is derived, not retrieved — I priced the add-ons on a different hotel's booking. The £2,637 headline is retrieved._

Read from each provider's own booking funnel, not from brand reputation.

| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Direct LGW–ACE flights | Yes                                                                                                | Yes                                                                                                           |

| **Hold luggage**       | **3 × 23kg, included, £0 to add.** Page states "Maximum 23kg per bag… outbound and return"         | **None.** Only "1 underseat bag per person (45 × 36 × 20 cm)". Checked bags **£101.98 each**; 3 = **£305.94** |

| ATOL                   | Yes                                                                                                | Yes                                                                                                           |

That is the trap in one line: loveholidays' £2,277 buys three people a week in Lanzarote with **one small under-seat bag each and no way of getting to the hotel**.

**easyJet's flights are also the better ones.** easyJet holidays departs **07:30**, landing Lanzarote **11:50** — you get the first day. loveholidays' cheapest departs **14:45**, landing **19:00** — that day is gone. Both return on the 19:55, into Gatwick at **23:55**, which is late with a 7-year-old; if that matters, loveholidays offered a BA return landing 23:25, and earlier easyJet returns exist but I did not price them.

## The bigger saving isn't the provider — it's the date

loveholidays' month calendar for the same hotel, same 7-night AI package, same party total:

| Fri 12 Feb | **Sat 13 Feb** | Sun 14 Feb | Mon 15 Feb | Tue 16 Feb | Wed 17 Feb |
| ---------- | -------------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| £1,977     | **£2,277**     | £1,557     | £1,467     | £1,257     | £1,197     |

**Sat 13 Feb is the most expensive day in the entire month.** Every other February date falls between £1,167 and £1,437. Moving to **Sunday 14 Feb** saves **£720** on that hotel and still covers the half-term week, returning Sun 21 Feb — you'd only need the Saturday, not a school day.

Choosing the right provider saves you ~£390. Choosing the right day saves you ~£720. Do both.

_Caveat: I verified this calendar on one hotel on one provider. Re-run it on easyJet before committing._

## Gaps — stated, not guessed

- **TUI is incomplete.** I confirmed by screenshot: Gatwick → Lanzarote, 13 Feb 2027, 7 nights, 2 adults + 1 child, All Inclusive filter applied → **38 holidays found**, all flying **Gatwick direct on easyJet aircraft**. Cheapest All Inclusive total _visible_ was **HL Paradise Island, Playa Blanca, £1,093pp / £3,277 total**; others seen were Los Zocos Impressive £3,647, Elba Lanzarote £3,963, Iberostar Selection Lanzarote Park £4,843. **But the list was sorted by "Recommended", not by price**, so a cheaper All Inclusive option may exist further down — £3,277 is a ceiling-ish observation, not a confirmed cheapest. **I did not verify TUI's bag or transfer inclusions.** Even so, TUI starts ~£1,000 above easyJet, and since TUI normally bundles bags and transfers already, there is little scope for it to close that gap. Retry URL (then set Board → All Inclusive and Sort by → price):
  `https://www.tui.co.uk/h/destinations/packages?airports[]=LGW&units[]=000329:DESTINATION&when=13-02-2027&noOfAdults=2&noOfChildren=1&childrenAge=7&duration=7115&sortBy=priceAsc`
- **Jet2holidays was not reached at all.** No figure. Jet2 does fly Lanzarote and bundles 22kg plus transfers, so it is the one genuine remaining chance of beating easyJet. Worth 10 minutes: `https://www.jet2holidays.com`
- **easyJet seat costs unconfirmed** — the site refuses to quote seats or extra bags this far ahead ("come back 30 days before your departure date"). Budget for it; I can't give a number.
- **loveholidays bag pricing** (£101.98/bag) applies to the easyJet-operated flights on that specific booking and varies by flight combination.
- No personal details or payment information were entered on any site, and nothing was booked.
