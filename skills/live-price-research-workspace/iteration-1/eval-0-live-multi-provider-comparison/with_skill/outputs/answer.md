# Lanzarote, Feb half term 2027 — who's actually cheapest

**Short answer: Jet2holidays, at £2,028 all-in for the three of you — and it's not close.**

Prices below are **live snapshots taken on 28 July 2026**. They move, sometimes within hours. Everything is a **party total for 2 adults + 1 child aged 7**, LGW→Lanzarote (ACE), depart Sat 13 Feb 2027, 7 nights, All Inclusive.

## The loveholidays trap, in their own words

This is verified, not inferred — I went into their checkout and read it off the extras page:

> **"Your booking includes: 1 underseat bag per person (45 x 36 x 20 cm)"**

That's the whole luggage allowance. A 45×36×20cm bag is a small rucksack. For a week's all-inclusive with a 7-year-old, you're adding:

| loveholidays extra                           | Price                                                                 | Verified how                                      |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 × 23kg checked bags                        | **£305.94**                                                           | Applied it — total went £2,277 → £2,582.94        |
| Return shuttle transfer (whole party)        | **£51**                                                               | Applied it — total went £2,582.94 → **£2,633.94** |
| _(optional)_ large overhead cabin bag, all 3 | from £170.94                                                          | Sold as "Priority Boarding"                       |
| _(optional)_ seat selection                  | from £7.99 per seat each way (£47.94 for 3 to sit together both ways) | —                                                 |

Note the transfer especially — loveholidays offers you a **"Continue without transfer"** button. Miss it and you land at Arrecife at 19:00 with no way to the hotel. Jet2, easyJet and TUI all include one.

They also charge a **£4.95 admin fee on every payment instalment** if you use their low-deposit or pay-monthly options, which the other three don't.

## Why Jet2 wins on more than price

I checked the things that could have flipped the ranking. None of them do — Jet2 wins on all of them.

**Flight times** (the usual place a cheap package hides its cost):

| Provider         | Out (Sat 13 Feb)            | Back (Sat 20 Feb) |
| ---------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **Jet2holidays** | 12:20 → 16:35               | 17:30 → **21:30** |
| easyJet holidays | 07:30 → 11:50 (early start) | 19:55 → **23:55** |
| loveholidays     | 14:45 → 19:00               | 19:55 → **23:55** |

With a 7-year-old, the difference between landing at Gatwick at 21:30 and 23:55 is real. Jet2 is the only one that gets you home before midnight, and it's also the cheapest. On loveholidays you can buy an earlier return (21:15 arrival) — it costs **+£180**, pushing their landed total to roughly £2,814.

**What's in the Jet2 price**, verbatim from their "Included in your holiday" panel:

> Return flights London Gatwick / (3) 10kg hand luggage / (3) 22kg baggage / Bluebay Lanzarote / All Inclusive / (1) One Bedroom apartment / Coach transfers / ATOL Protected

The "(3)" is the bit that matters — **the child gets a full 22kg hold bag and a 10kg cabin bag too**, not a reduced allowance.

**Deposits:** Jet2 £180 total (balance due 10 weeks out, ~early Dec 2026) · easyJet £180 total (balance 28 days out) · TUI £225 · loveholidays £87.

## The one thing that could change this recommendation

**Jet2's £2,028 depends on a Free Child Place.** Every one of their top five results carries "Includes / Free Child Place x1" — the child's package cost is £0, which is why their per-person figure is £1,014 (£2,028 ÷ 2 paying adults) rather than £676.

Free Child Places are capacity-controlled and get withdrawn without warning, especially on a half-term date. **If it goes, the price roughly doubles.** This is the single reason to book sooner rather than later — at £180 deposit with the balance not due until December, holding the price costs you very little.

easyJet holidays also runs Free Child Places in Lanzarote, so if Jet2's disappears, check whether easyJet's £2,247 has one attached before assuming the whole market has moved.

## Worth knowing: 13 Feb is the single worst day to fly

I pulled the whole February 2027 calendar from two providers rather than just your date. Your date is the peak of the peak.

**loveholidays (LIVVO Morromar, 7 nights, total for 3):**

| Sat 6  | Fri 12 | **Sat 13** | Sun 14     | Sat 20 |
| ------ | ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ------ |
| £1,287 | £1,977 | **£2,277** | **£1,557** | £1,257 |

**Jet2holidays (cheapest AI, 7 nights, total for 3):**

| Sat 6  | Thu 11 | **Sat 13** | Sun 14 | Tue 16 |
| ------ | ------ | ---------- | ------ | ------ |
| £1,548 | £1,722 | **£2,028** | £2,008 | £1,618 |

The interesting one is **Sunday 14 February**. Seven nights from Sun 14 returns Sun 21 Feb — your daughter still misses zero school days, since half term is the week of 15–19 Feb either way. You lose one day of holiday.

- On **loveholidays** that swap is worth **£720 off the headline** (£2,277 → £1,557). Adding the same bags and transfer would put it near £1,914, though I priced those extras against the 13 Feb booking and did not re-verify them for the 14th.
- On **Jet2** the same swap saves only £20 — their half-term pricing covers both days.

**If a Sunday departure is acceptable to you, it is worth 15 minutes of your time to re-run all four providers on 14 Feb 2027 before booking.** It could be the largest single saving available, larger than the provider choice itself.

## What I'd do

1. **Book Jet2holidays, Bluebay Lanzarote, £2,028**, unless the Sunday check below beats it. £180 deposit, balance ~early December. Best price, best flight times, everything included, ATOL 9618.
2. **Before you pay, re-run Sun 14 Feb 2027** on Jet2 and loveholidays. If loveholidays lands under ~£1,950 all-in _with bags and transfers added_, it's worth the worse flight times.
3. **Do not book TUI for this trip.** They're reselling easyJet flights at a £1,096 premium on an identical hotel.
4. **If you do use loveholidays for anything**, the extras page is the only place the truth appears — the results-page price is flights and hotel only.
