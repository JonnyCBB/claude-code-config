## Flags

**1. The dates and the year don't line up.** The booking rules say **March 2027**, and both quotes are dated Feb 2027. But the weekday labels on the sheet only work for **March 2026** — 9, 16 and 23 March are Mondays in 2026, but Tuesdays in 2027. (Same for "week commencing Monday 30 March": that's 2026; in 2027 the last week starts Monday 29 March.) Since today is 28 July 2026, March 2026 is in the past, so the year has to be 2027 and the weekday labels are wrong.

This changes rows 1–3: if those dates are the **Tuesdays** they actually are in 2027, there's no Monday surcharge and each is **£1,392**, not £1,536. I've priced them as Mondays above because that's what the sheet says. **Please confirm which you meant.** It doesn't change the venue ranking either way.

**2. Alderman's Court is unpriced and I have not guessed at it.** "From £1,100 per day" is an advertised floor, not a quote, and the site says March midweek availability is limited. At face value it would undercut Kingsgate by ~£292/day, so it's worth chasing — but it also only holds 25, leaving one seat of headroom against your 24, and nobody has spoken to them. The voicemail from 5 Feb needs a follow-up call before this row can be filled in.

**3. Wharf's AV is a choice, not a fixed number.** I costed projector + screen + mics (£275/day) to match what Kingsgate bundles. Dropping mics saves £95/day; dropping the second tea/coffee serving saves £108. Even at Wharf's absolute floor — room + projector/screen + lunch, no refreshments, no mics — it's £1,706/day, still £314 above Kingsgate's full-service £1,392. Wharf cannot be made to win on price.
