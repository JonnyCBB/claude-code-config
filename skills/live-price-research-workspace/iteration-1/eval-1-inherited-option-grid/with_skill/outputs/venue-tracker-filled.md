## ⚠️ Two problems with the grid, before the numbers

**1. The day-of-week labels are from a 2026 calendar.** The rules say March **2027** and the quotes are dated Feb 2027, but in March 2027 the Mondays are 1, 8, 15, 22, 29 — so "Mon 9 Mar", "Mon 16 Mar", "Mon 23 Mar" don't exist. Those dates were Mondays in March **2026**. This is not cosmetic: Kingsgate charges **+£6 per person on Mondays**, so the weekday decides the price. Each row below is priced under both readings.

**2. The grid is 5 of ~28 permitted single-day options, and it excludes the cheapest region.** See the option-space section below.

## The option space the rules actually permit

Rules: any Mon–Thu in March 2027, no Fridays, avoid the week commencing Mon 30 Mar _(no such Monday in 2027 — read as **w/c Mon 29 Mar**, so 29/30/31 Mar are out)_, 1 or 2 consecutive days, same venue for two-day, 20–30 attendees, ≥3 weeks lead time.

That gives **16 permitted single days** × 2 quotable venues = **32 single-day options**, plus **12 permitted consecutive pairs** × 2 venues = **24 two-day options**. The grid priced 5.

Permitted single days: 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 15, 16, 17, 18, 22, 23, 24, 25 March 2027.
