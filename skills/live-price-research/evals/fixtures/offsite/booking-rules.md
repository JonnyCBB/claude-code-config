# Offsite booking rules (from Ops)

- **Any weekday, Monday to Thursday, during March 2027.** Fridays are not permitted (too many people take them as leave).
- **One or two consecutive days.** Two-day offsites need the same venue on both days.
- **Avoid the week commencing Monday 30 March** — year-end audit, finance team unavailable.
- Minimum 20 attendees, maximum 30. We have 24 confirmed.
- Venue must provide AV and be able to cater lunch. Refreshments can be added separately if not bundled.
- Anything over **£4,000 total** needs director sign-off, which takes about a fortnight, so flag it early.
- Book at least 3 weeks ahead.
