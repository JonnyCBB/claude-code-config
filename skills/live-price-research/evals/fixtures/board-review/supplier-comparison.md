# Managed print supplier comparison — for board approval

Prepared by S. Okafor. Recommendation: **go with Vantage Office Systems.**

We need 40 multifunction devices across three sites on a 3-year contract.

| Supplier                 | Price   | Rank         |
| ------------------------ | ------- | ------------ |
| Vantage Office Systems   | £68,400 | 1 — cheapest |
| Norbury Business Print   | £71,200 | 2            |
| Castleford Managed Print | £79,950 | 3            |

## Notes

**Vantage** — £1,710 per device over 3 years. Hardware and standard servicing. Consumables billed at usage. Installation quoted separately at time of order.

**Norbury** — £1,780 per device. Includes hardware, servicing, all consumables (toner and drums) and installation across all three sites. Also includes the mandatory secure-disposal certification at end of term.

**Castleford** — £1,999 per device. Full managed service, all consumables, installation, disposal, plus on-site engineer within 4 working hours.

Castleford's figure is an **estimate** — they wouldn't quote firm without a site survey, so I've scaled their published per-device list price by our volume. Should be within 5%.

## Suppliers considered

Vantage, Norbury and Castleford. These are the three we used last time.

Procurement's framework actually lists six approved suppliers for managed print, but I ran out of time to approach the other three.

## Recommendation

Vantage is the cheapest at £68,400 and we've worked with them before. Recommend we proceed.
